01 · About

A simple token foundation with transparent onchain rules.

SUNV was deployed as a fixed-supply ERC-20. The verified contract exposes standard ERC-20 transfer and approval functionality and does not expose a public mint function that can increase the original supply.

The project is in its launch-stage development phase. Future ecosystem features, integrations and use cases will be published as they are built and verified rather than presented as completed before they exist.
