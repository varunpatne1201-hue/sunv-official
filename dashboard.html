05 · Current limitations

What the dashboard does not hide.

Source verification and public data improve transparency but do not remove financial, technical or custody risk.

!

Shallow liquidity

Small trades can materially change the displayed price and FDV.

!

Concentrated reserve

Most SUNV remains controlled by the project deployer wallet.

!

LP not presented as locked

The current Uniswap v4 position remains under LP-owner control.
