01 · Purpose

A transparent foundation for a developing onchain ecosystem.

The near-term purpose of SUNV is to provide a simple, verifiable token foundation around which the SUN ecosystem can be developed. The project is intentionally separating the token's existing onchain properties from future utility so that planned ideas are not presented as completed products.

Development will initially focus on public transparency infrastructure, reliable project information, community channels and practical tools that make SUNV easier to verify and understand. Additional utility will be introduced only after it is built, tested and documented.

02 · Design principles

Rules for how SUNV should develop.

These principles are project commitments for communication and development. They do not change the ERC-20 contract itself.

01

Verify before marketing

Features, integrations, locks and allocations are described as implemented only when they can be verified.

02

Fixed-supply clarity

The original 100M supply is fixed. Total supply, project reserves and circulating supply are treated as separate concepts.

03

Transparent reserves

Project-controlled wallets and material reserve or liquidity changes should be publicly disclosed.

04

No return promises

SUNV is a volatile digital asset. Project communications will not promise profits, price targets or guaranteed appreciation.

04 · Milestone roadmap

Progress is milestone-based, not hype-based.

01

Foundation — completed

Deploy the fixed-supply contract, verify source code, test transfers, create the SUNV/USDG Uniswap v4 market, verify two-way swaps, launch the official website and establish canonical project links.

02

Trust infrastructure — active

Publish current reserve and liquidity disclosures, complete external token metadata reviews, document treasury policy, improve custody practices, establish community moderation and publish consistent project updates.

03

SUNV transparency dashboard — initial version live

The first public dashboard combines live DEX Screener market data, Blockscout holder data, canonical contract information, project-controlled wallet links, reserve disclosures and independent source links in one place. Additional integrations will be added only where reliable data sources are available.

04

Holder Hub utility — beta live

The first practical SUNV utility is now live in beta: a read-only holder tool that verifies SUNV balances against the canonical contract and provides wallet-level portfolio context without requiring signatures, approvals or transactions. Broader token-gated access, rewards, payments, governance or partner integrations remain exploratory.

05

Ecosystem integrations — future

Evaluate integrations with wallets, analytics providers, applications or community tools based on actual usage and technical fit. No exchange listing, partnership or third-party integration is guaranteed.

06

Decentralization review — future

As the project matures, evaluate multisig custody, reserve controls, vesting or locking structures and community participation mechanisms. Any adopted structure will be documented and verifiable onchain.

06 · Treasury & liquidity policy direction

Use reserves deliberately and disclose material changes.

The project reserve is intended to remain a controlled reserve while treasury, custody and future allocation rules are developed. Large reserve movements should have a documented project purpose rather than being represented as organic market activity.

Liquidity should be expanded according to genuine market needs and project capacity, not solely to create a larger headline valuation. The project will avoid representing self-trades, controlled wallets or artificial activity as community adoption.

Before any formal vesting, locking, multisig or distribution program is announced as active, its implementation should be independently verifiable.

07 · Risks & limitations

Early-stage means high uncertainty.

SUNV can lose value, and no part of this litepaper is a promise of financial performance.

!

Shallow liquidity

Small buys or sells can cause large price movements and significant slippage.

!

Reserve concentration

Most supply remains under project control, creating concentration and custody risk until stronger controls are implemented.

!

Execution risk

Planned ecosystem features may change, be delayed or never be implemented.

!

Third-party risk

Wallets, bridges, DEXs, explorers and analytics services are outside SUNV's control.

!

Smart-contract risk

Source verification improves transparency but does not eliminate software, integration or ecosystem risks.

!

Regulatory risk

Digital-asset rules and platform requirements can change across jurisdictions.
