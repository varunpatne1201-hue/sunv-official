Supply & treasury transparency.

This page describes the current onchain distribution of the fixed 100,000,000 SUNV supply and the project's launch-stage liquidity position. It separates implemented facts from future plans.

Snapshot date: September 22, 2026. Wallet and AMM balances can change after this snapshot. Use Blockscout, Uniswap and GeckoTerminal for current onchain data.

01 · Current supply

100 million SUNV. No additional minting.

04 · Reserve status

What is implemented today.

Project reserve: approximately 99.94% of the fixed supply remains in the original deployer-controlled reserve wallet.

Vesting: no formal onchain vesting schedule has been implemented for the project reserve as of this snapshot.

Liquidity lock: the Uniswap v4 liquidity position is not currently presented as locked.

Future allocation: allocation targets discussed during project planning are not shown here as completed allocations. Future distributions will be described as implemented only after the corresponding onchain transfers or contracts exist.

05 · Verify independently

Use onchain sources.

SUNV market capitalization shown by wallets or charting sites is a price-derived estimate, not cash held by the project or liquidity available for redemptions. With shallow liquidity, displayed valuation can move sharply from small trades.
