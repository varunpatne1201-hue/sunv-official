# SUNV Litepaper v0.1
**Date:** September 22, 2026

## Executive summary
SUNV is a fixed-supply ERC-20 token on Robinhood Chain and the base asset for a developing SUN ecosystem. This document distinguishes what is implemented today from future plans.

## Current implementation
- Name: Sun
- Symbol: SUNV
- Contract: `0x22fd16577ba869A7df77F4280ae08c65BB03111d`
- Network: Robinhood Chain mainnet
- Chain ID: 4663
- Total supply: 100,000,000 SUNV
- Decimals: 18
- Source-verified contract
- SUNV/USDG market on Uniswap v4
- Public market tracking on GeckoTerminal, DEXTools and DEX Screener
- Official website, X account and Telegram community

At this stage SUNV's implemented token functionality is that of a standard transferable ERC-20 with a live decentralized market. Broader ecosystem utility is under development.

## Development principles
1. Verify before marketing.
2. Separate total supply, project reserves and circulating supply.
3. Disclose project-controlled wallets and material reserve/liquidity changes.
4. Do not promise returns or price appreciation.
5. Do not represent controlled activity as organic adoption.

## Milestone roadmap
### Phase 1 — Foundation — completed
Contract deployment, source verification, transfer testing, Uniswap v4 SUNV/USDG market, two-way swap verification, official website and canonical project links.

### Phase 2 — Trust infrastructure — active
Reserve/liquidity disclosures, external metadata reviews, treasury policy, improved custody practices, community moderation and consistent public updates.

### Phase 3 — SUNV transparency dashboard — planned
A public dashboard bringing together canonical contract data, project-controlled wallet links, liquidity references, market-data links, holder information and project disclosures.

### Phase 4 — Utility prototype — exploratory
Design and test the first practical SUN ecosystem feature that can use SUNV in a measurable way. Token-gated access, rewards, payments, governance and partner integrations are exploratory until specifications and working implementations are published.

### Phase 5 — Ecosystem integrations — future
Evaluate wallet, analytics, application and community-tool integrations based on real usage and technical fit.

### Phase 6 — Decentralization review — future
Evaluate multisig custody, reserve controls, vesting/locking structures and community participation mechanisms. Any adopted structure will be documented and verifiable onchain.

## Current limitations
- Formal reserve vesting: not implemented.
- Liquidity lock: not represented as implemented.
- Decentralized governance: not implemented.
- Guaranteed allocation schedule: not implemented.
- Guaranteed third-party listings or integrations: none.

## Risks
SUNV is early-stage and carries shallow-liquidity, reserve-concentration, execution, third-party, smart-contract and regulatory risks. Digital assets can lose value. No return or price target is guaranteed.
