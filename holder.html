Utility · v1.4 Beta

Your SUNV, verified onchain.

Enter any public EVM wallet address or connect a browser wallet. Holder Hub reads the SUNV ERC-20 balance from Robinhood Chain, shows recent transfers, calculates wallet-level SUNV analytics, combines the balance with live market data, and creates a shareable live-check link. It does not request a signature, approval or transaction.

Wallet address

Read-only: no wallet signature, token approval, transaction or private key is requested. A connected wallet only shares its public address with this page.

Why this is useful

A real token utility without asking holders to spend anything.

This beta gives SUNV holders and observers a direct way to verify holdings against the canonical contract while keeping the interaction non-custodial and read-only.
